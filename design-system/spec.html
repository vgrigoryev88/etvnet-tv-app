Excerpt from the component specification

How components work and talk to each other

The full specification covers every component of the etvnet TV app: its layout, states, motion and the rules it shares with other components. Two parts are shown here: the side menu, and long-press quick actions on cards. Sizes are for the 1920 × 1080 canvas.

1. Side menu2. Long press · quick actions

1. Side menu

A vertical panel on the left edge with the app's sections. It has two widths, and focus is either in the menu or in the page content. The menu and the page never hold focus at the same time: one hands it to the other.

Widths

ViewWidthWhat is shown
Expanded384Full logo, icons and section names
Collapsed (rail)136Compact logo and icons only

Structure

  • Panel padding 48 on top, left and bottom. Logo 64 high at the top, first item 48 below it.
  • Items are 64 high with a 16 gap (80 step). Search and Settings are pinned to the bottom, 48 from the edge, whatever the number of items above.
  • Item: full pill 302 × 64 when expanded, 64 × 64 circle when collapsed. Icon 56, label Inter Medium 28/32.

Item states

StateWhenLook
RestedAny other itemNo fill. In the collapsed rail the icon is at 50%.
SelectedThe current sectionTonal pill #302C2C, icon at 100%. Never dimmed.
FocusedFocus is on this itemRed pill #D7192D

When focus is in the menu but not on the current section, both pills are visible: red on the focused item, tonal on the current section. The two pills are separate layers that slide to their item, so they move rather than blink.

Which width each screen uses

The viewer picks the format in Settings → Navigation panel, where Movies, Series and TV Archive can also be hidden. Hidden items leave no gap and the arrows skip them.

How focus moves between the menu and the page

SituationResult
Focus in content, Left from the left edge (first column, first chip, back chip)Focus moves to the menu, on the current section. The menu opens.
Back at the top level of a main screenSame as above
Focus in menu, Right or BackFocus returns to the exact place it left in the content. The menu collapses if the screen uses the rail.
Focus in menu, OK on another sectionThe section opens with a crossfade, focus goes to its content, the menu takes the new screen's width.
Focus in menu, OK on the current sectionFocus simply returns to the content.
  • From the rail the menu opens over the content; the content never shifts. Everything to the right of the panel dims to 70%.
  • The content focus ring hides while the menu has focus, so only one red element is on screen.
  • Up and Down do not wrap. Left does nothing in the menu.

2. Long press · quick actions

Starts a title or adds it to favourites straight from its card, without opening the title page. On live channels it starts the channel, opens its TV guide or adds it to favourites. It is an accelerator: every action is also available the regular way, so there is no on-screen hint.

Input

RemoteResult
Short OK on a cardAs before: opens the title page or starts the channel. Fires on release.
Hold OK for 600 ms or moreOpens the quick actions. Key auto-repeat is ignored, so it opens once.
Up / DownMove between buttons
OKRun the button's action. A held OK is ignored; only a new press counts.
BackClose with no action. Focus stays on the card.

Where it works and where it does not

  • Content cards on Archive rows, Movies, Series, categories, channel archive, title pages, search results, Favourites, Watched, collection and person pages.
  • Live channels in the Live TV grid and list.
  • Not on links: TV Archive channel tiles, category tiles, collections, people and See-all cards. A hold there acts as a normal OK.

What the window offers

CardButtons
TitleStart watching or Continue watching, then Add to / Remove from favourites
Live channelStart watching, TV guide (focused on open), Add to / Remove from favourites

The play button works at the level of the card it was opened on, using the same logic as the title page button:

Card levelNot startedStarted
MovieMovie · 128 min45 min left

ShowLatest episodeWhere the viewer stopped in the whole show

EpisodeEpisode meta12 min left

OK on the play button closes the window and opens the player directly; the title page is skipped.

How it talks to other components

  • Card: keeps its normal focused state, ring and bright title underneath. No extra outline.
  • Title: not repeated in the window when the card has its own caption. Only a channel tile in the Live TV grid, which has no caption, gets the channel name as the window title.
  • Favourites button: the same state as on the title page. Toggling it switches the label and icon, shows a toast, and closes the window after about 260 ms.
  • Toast: Added to favourites / Removed from favourites; for channels, Channel added to favourites / Channel removed from favourites.
  • Focus colour: red means focus. While the window is open, only the card focus and the focused button are red.

Position and look

  • To the right of the card, 24 from the focus ring. If it does not fit before the right margin, it goes to the left. Vertically centred on the card itself (its caption is not counted), kept 32 inside the screen.
  • Wide cards (Live TV list rows) get the window below the row, or above it near the bottom of the screen.
  • Window 600 wide, surface background, hairline border, radius 24, padding 32, buttons 16 apart. The rest of the screen, including the menu, dims to 60%.
  • Opens in 200 ms (fade and scale 0.96 → 1 from the side facing the card), closes in 120 ms.

Changed after the last usability test round (Nov 2026): the channel window first had only TV guide and Favourites. Viewers sometimes held OK by accident or simply wanted to watch, so Start watching was added to the channel window.
